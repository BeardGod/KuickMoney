# Lessons log

Short post-mortems written after picks are graded. The analyst reads the most recent ones before every card.

- **2026-10-04**: Eagles +3.5 lost 24-20 after leading 20-10 in the 4th. Fading lopsided public money on a road favorite was reasonable, but Philly was missing its top two WRs and the offense stalled late; when the dog is missing its best skill players, the hook alone isn't enough.
- **2026-10-04**: Titans +11.5 won (lost 24-18). Fading 82% public money on a double-digit favorite whose offensive line was depleted (both centers out) worked: injured OL limits a favorite's ability to run up the score.
- **2026-10-04**: Commanders +4.5 lost 30-13. Mariota's one good game was over-weighted and McLaurin's absence under-weighted. A backup QB missing his No. 1 receiver is a bigger downgrade than the market move suggests; don't back that side on public-fade alone.
- **2026-10-04**: Brewers ML -142 won 4-3 on a walk-off. The starting-pitcher FIP gap (3.32 vs 4.13) at home was a sound basis.
- **2026-10-05**: Falcons +2.5 won outright 45-24 and the line closed +1.5 (beat the close by a point). A line moving against the money side plus a depleted front seven against a strong run game was the best signal this week.
- **2026-10-05**: Yankees ML -128 lost 5-2 with four errors. The ace-vs-small-sample starter edge was real but this was a road game against a team already up 1-0; defense and playoff bullpen usage swamp a single starter edge. Size these at 0.5-1u.
- **2026-10-05**: Northern Ireland +150 drew 0-0 (missed late penalty). In soccer three-way markets a strong clean-sheet team vs a non-scoring team often ends in a draw; for low-scoring matchups consider draw-no-bet or the under instead of the win.
- **2026-10-05**: Sweden -125 won 1-0 at Romania. Form gap plus a depleted opponent (key center-back suspended, two forwards out) held up.
- **2026-10-06**: Southern Miss/Troy Under 49.5 lost badly (55-34, 89 points). Wrong assumption: a backup QB on the weaker team does not make a game low-scoring; the stronger team can score 50+ and the weak defense gets exposed. Don't bet unders on a backup-QB angle when the favorite has a big offense; consider the favorite's team total over instead.
- **2026-10-07**: Kennesaw State +3 won (lost 27-26 on a FG with 51s left). Line moved toward the dog off -3.5 and the model had KSU as a slight favorite; the +3 covered by 2. Note: KSU started Rickie Collins at QB, not Amari Odom as the report assumed, so verify starting QBs before kickoff for college games - the result was right but part of the reasoning was stale.
